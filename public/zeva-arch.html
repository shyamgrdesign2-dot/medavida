4 Global systems

Patterns that run app-wide

The reusable models every screen inherits.

Navigation

Five persistent bottom-nav tabs (Home · Cards · Activity · Insights · More). Deeper surfaces open as full-screen pushed overlays; quick choices as drag-to-dismiss bottom sheets.

Card system

A metallic, gyroscope-reactive card is the hero object — it tilts to device motion. Two cards: an operating debit and a credit card. They are the only funding sources — every send, bill and payment draws from one of them; there are no internal wallets or account-splitting.

Money model

Two directions, one ledger: money in through Zeva Pay (2% · instant), money out by ACH and bill pay funded from the debit / credit card. Every dollar is tagged with patient ID + Rx.

Theming

A full light and dark system with theme-aware accents, elevation and the brand mark. A first-run picker chooses the look before the splash; light mode matches the MedaVida brand.

Explainers

Every operations screen opens with a collapsible "what is this" card — an X shrinks it to an info chip that re-expands on tap — so a PA or new teammate understands each feature at a glance.

Roles & approvals

Role-based access shapes what each member sees and does, and links a spend-capped card to whoever needs one; high-value money movement routes to owner approval.

States

Every flow resolves to a clear success moment; empty, loading, and guarded (insufficient-funds, over-limit, missing-W-9) states are designed, not afterthoughts.

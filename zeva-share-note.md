# Zeva — shared for review

Hi team,

Sharing **Zeva**, the money layer for clinics (a MedaVida product) — the app that moves,
tracks, and separates every dollar a clinic earns and spends. Three things to look at:

### 1 · Live prototype
**https://zeva-medavida.vercel.app/**
A clickable, mobile-first prototype. Best viewed on a phone. Tap once on load to enable the
motion cards. Ships in dark mode, with a full light theme (switch under **More → Appearance**).

### 2 · Information architecture
**https://zeva-medavida.vercel.app/zeva-arch.html**
The complete map of the app — a structure flowchart, a screen-by-screen breakdown of what
lives on every surface and what you can do there, the key task flows, and the global systems.
Opens in any browser, full width, no login.

### 3 · Product brief
Attached — **zeva-product-brief.md**. What Zeva is, the money model (Zeva Pay: patient pays →
2% fee → clinic gets the net instantly), the design references, and every section's features.

---

**In one line:** patients pay from a card on file and the clinic receives the net **instantly**;
outward, the clinic runs its cards, bills, payroll, reserves, and books from the same place.

Happy to walk through any of it live. Thanks!
